## Order processing & dispatch (Shipping Policy page me add karo)

- **Prepaid orders** (UPI, card, netbanking) are confirmed instantly and are packed and dispatched
  ahead of Cash on Delivery orders in our processing queue.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD) orders** go through an order-verification step and are dispatched after
  prepaid orders, so they may take slightly longer to reach you.
- Delivery timelines depend on your PIN code and the courier partner; estimated timelines are not guaranteed.
- An online-payment discount, where applicable, is shown at checkout before you pay.
