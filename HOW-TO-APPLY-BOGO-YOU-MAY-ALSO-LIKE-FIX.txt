BOGO PRODUCT PAGE PAR "YOU MAY ALSO LIKE" MISSING — FIX
=========================================================

Issue
-----
Jis product par live "Buy X Get Y" (BOGO) offer hota tha, uss page par
sirf "Complete your Buy 2 Get 1 Free offer" carousel dikhta tha.
"You may also like" (same category ke generic related products) us page
par bilkul render hi nahi hota tha.

Wajah
-----
app/product/[slug]/product-detail.tsx mein ek hi <RelatedProducts>
component call hota tha. Jab BOGO collection ke products mil jaate
(overrideProducts), to wahi ek carousel BOGO offer dikhata tha aur
generic "You may also like" (score-based same-category matches) poori
tarah replace/skip ho jaata tha — kyunki component sirf ek list render
karta hai, dono nahi.

Fix
---
Ab jab BOGO products mil jaate hain, to DO alag <RelatedProducts>
render hote hain:
  1) Pehla: BOGO offer wala carousel ("Complete your ... offer") — jaisa
     pehle tha, waisa hi.
  2) Dusra (naya): plain <RelatedProducts current={product}
     allProducts={products} /> — koi overrideProducts nahi, isliye ye
     apna default title "You may also like" ke saath generic
     same-category matches dikhata hai.

Order same rehta hai jaisa aapne chaha:
  BOGO offer carousel -> You may also like -> Recently Viewed

Agar us product par koi live BOGO offer nahi hai (bogoCollectionProducts
empty), to page bilkul pehle jaisa hi behave karega — sirf ek
"You may also like" carousel, koi extra/duplicate section nahi.

File jo change hui
-------------------
  app/product/[slug]/product-detail.tsx

Kaise apply karein
-------------------
1) Is zip ko apne project root me extract karo (E-Commerce-ethnic folder
   ke andar) — wahi folder structure hai jo aapke project me hai
   (app/product/[slug]/product-detail.tsx), isliye extract karte hi wo
   apne aap sahi jagah replace ho jaayegi. "Yes, overwrite" karna.
2) Terminal me:
     git add app/product/[slug]/product-detail.tsx
     git commit -m "Show You may also like alongside BOGO offer on product page"
     git push
3) Deploy ho jaane ke baad kisi BOGO product page pe check karo:
   Complete your Buy X Get Y offer -> You may also like -> Recently Viewed

Verified: `npx tsc --noEmit` clean pass hua is file pe.

Alternate: bogo-you-may-also-like-fix.diff bhi diya hai agar aap
`git apply` se seedha patch lagana chahein.
