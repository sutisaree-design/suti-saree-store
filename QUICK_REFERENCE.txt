Purchase (actual sale) ........... 100 points ★★★★★
Checkout Started ................. 30 points ★★★
Add to Cart ...................... 10 points ★★
Wishlist Added ................... 5 points  ★
Product Clicked .................. 2 points  ★
Product Viewed ................... 1 point   (baseline)

Time Decay:
├─ Last 7 days ................... 100% weight (full points)
├─ 7-30 days old ................. 70% weight
├─ 30-90 days old ................ 30% weight
└─ Older than 90 days ............ 0% weight (ignored)

  Edit: lib/popularity-rank-server.ts
  Look for: const WEIGHTS = { ... }
  Example: Increase 'purchase' from 100 to 200 to prioritize sales more

Change Time Windows:
  Edit: lib/popularity-rank-server.ts
  Look for: const WEIGHT_DECAY = { ... }
  Example: Change recent_days from 7 to 14 for longer "recent" period

Change Badge Threshold:
  Edit: app/shop/shop-content.tsx
  Look for: rankIndex < 10
  Example: Change to rankIndex < 5 to only badge top 5 products

Change Refresh Frequency:
  Edit: components/category/category-toolbar-grid.tsx
  Look for: 10 * 60 * 1000
  Example: Change to 5 * 60 * 1000 for 5-minute refresh
